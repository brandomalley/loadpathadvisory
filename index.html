Load Path Advisory

Specialty engineering scopes, placed with the right firm.

Most design firms have work that shows up on a project a few times a year and sits outside their core practice. Load Path Advisory connects those firms with specialty consultants who do that work every day.

What we source

  • Arc flash and NFPA 70E studies
  • Short circuit and protective device coordination
  • Relay protection
  • Power system design and utility interconnection

How it works

We learn which firms handle which scopes in-house and which send them out. When a project needs something outside a firm's practice, we make an introduction to a consultant who fits it.

There is no cost to the design firm for the introduction.

Load Path Advisory is a project sourcing practice of EPM Collaborative.
